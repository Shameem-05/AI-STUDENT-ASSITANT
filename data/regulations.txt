COLLEGE ACADEMIC REGULATIONS

1. Attendance Policy
Students must maintain a minimum of 75% attendance in each course to be eligible to sit for the end-semester examination. Students with attendance between 65% and 74% may apply for condonation through the Head of Department, subject to a valid medical or personal reason supported by documents. Students below 65% attendance are detained and must repeat the course.

2. Examination Eligibility
A student is eligible to appear for the end-semester examination only if:
- Attendance requirement (Section 1) is satisfied.
- All internal assessments for the course have been completed.
- All library dues and hostel/mess dues are cleared.

3. Grading System
The college follows a 10-point Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) system.
Grade O = 10 points (90-100 marks)
Grade A+ = 9 points (80-89 marks)
Grade A = 8 points (70-79 marks)
Grade B+ = 7 points (60-69 marks)
Grade B = 6 points (50-59 marks)
Grade C = 5 points (40-49 marks)
Grade F = 0 points (below 40 marks, Fail)

4. Revaluation and Retest
Students who are not satisfied with their internal or end-semester marks may apply for revaluation within 10 days of result publication by paying the prescribed fee at the Examination Cell. Students who fail in a course may apply for a supplementary/retest in the following semester.

5. Backlog Policy
A student can carry a maximum of 3 backlog subjects at a time to be promoted to the next year. Students with more than 3 backlogs will not be promoted and must clear the backlog subjects before proceeding.

6. Code of Conduct
Ragging in any form is strictly prohibited and punishable under the college anti-ragging policy, which may lead to suspension or expulsion. Use of unfair means during examinations (copying, use of unauthorized material, impersonation) leads to immediate cancellation of the exam and disciplinary action by the Academic Committee.

7. Leave of Absence
Students requiring leave for more than 3 consecutive working days must submit a leave application to the Head of Department along with supporting documents (medical certificate, event participation letter, etc.) at least 2 days in advance, except in emergencies.

8. Scholarship and Fee Concession
Merit scholarships are awarded to the top 5% of students in each department based on previous semester CGPA. Fee concessions for economically weaker sections are processed through the Dean of Student Affairs office at the start of each academic year.
