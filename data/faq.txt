FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Q: How do I apply for a bonafide certificate?
A: Submit a request through the Student Portal under "Certificate Requests" or apply in person at the Administrative Office with your ID card. Certificates are usually issued within 3 working days.

Q: What is the procedure to get a transcript?
A: Transcripts can be requested from the Examination Cell by filling out Form TR-1, available on the college website, and paying the transcript fee. Processing takes 5-7 working days.

Q: How can I reset my student portal password?
A: Click "Forgot Password" on the login page and follow the OTP verification sent to your registered email or phone number. If the issue persists, contact the IT Helpdesk.

Q: Where can I check the academic calendar?
A: The academic calendar, including semester start/end dates, exam schedules, and holidays, is published on the college website under "Academics > Academic Calendar" and updated on the notice board each semester.

Q: How do I apply for a hostel room?
A: Hostel applications open at the start of each academic year through the Hostel Office. Students need to submit the hostel application form along with the fee receipt and a medical fitness certificate.

Q: Who do I contact for scholarship-related queries?
A: Scholarship queries are handled by the Dean of Student Affairs office, room 204, Admin Block. Application windows are announced via email and notice board at the start of each semester.

Q: How do I join a student club or committee?
A: Club recruitment happens during the first month of each semester. Interested students can register interest through the Student Activity Portal or contact the respective club's faculty coordinator.

Q: What should I do if I miss an internal test due to illness?
A: Submit a medical certificate to your Head of Department within 3 days of the missed test to be considered for a make-up test, subject to department approval.

Q: How do I raise a complaint about a faculty member or facility?
A: Complaints can be submitted anonymously or with identity through the Grievance Redressal Portal on the college website, or in writing to the Grievance Redressal Committee.

Q: Can I change my elective subject after the semester begins?
A: Elective changes are only permitted within the first 2 weeks of the semester, subject to seat availability, by submitting an Elective Change Request Form to the department office.
